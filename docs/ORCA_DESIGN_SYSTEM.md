## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an ultra-refined, high-precision geospatial operational interface built for maritime intelligence, naval domain awareness, and deep-sea route optimization. 

The aesthetic fuses organic coastal calm with disciplined technical clarity. It pairs an organic, soothing sage-mist canvas with stark white architectural floating surfaces, deep naval charcoal anchoring, and focused maritime amber telemetry. The emotional impression is sovereign authority, situational clarity, and calm under operational scale.

Key pillars:
- **Quiet Precision:** Data density without visual noise. Surfaces float cleanly above terrain and oceanographic layers.
- **Architectural Aerodynamics:** Sculpted pill silhouettes, continuous smooth corner radiuses, and tactile floating docks.
- **Technical Warmth:** Cool atmospheric sage and seafoam balances against warm beacon amber and luminescent track highlights.

## Colors

The palette references maritime operational environments: coastal sea mist, chart paper, oceanic depth markers, and warning beacons.

- **Canvas & Atmospheric Tones:** 
  - Primary Base Canvas: `#D5E0D8` (Soft Sage)
  - Secondary Underlay / Map Mist: `#CCD9CF`
  - Elevated Pure Surface: `#FFFFFF`
  - Translucent Surface Glass: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.78)`
- **Structural Charcoals:** 
  - Primary Deep Anchor: `#121714` (Deep Sea Charcoal)
  - Secondary Structural Carbon: `#1C231F`
  - Deep Surface Overlay: `rgba(18, 23, 20, 0.04)` to `rgba(18, 23, 20, 0.08)`
- **Coastal Slates (Midtones):** 
  - Slate Muted: `#526058`
  - Slate Subtle: `#718277`
  - Hairline Rim Borders: `rgba(82, 96, 88, 0.15)`
- **Maritime Beacon Accents:** 
  - Primary Beacon Amber: `#D97706`
  - Active Pulse Gold: `#F59E0B`
  - Solar Highlight: `#EAB308`
  - Alert Critical: `#DC2626`
  - Nominal Marine Cyan: `#0D9488`

## Typography

Typography prioritizes fast tactical scanning and high legibility across vector charts and multi-layered geospatial viewports. 

- **Primary Typeface:** Plus Jakarta Sans is applied across headlines, interface labels, and operational copy. Its sculpted geometric geometry pairs cleanly with round UI elements.
- **Telemetry & AIS Metric Typeface:** JetBrains Mono is utilized specifically for latitude/longitude, MMSI vessel identifiers, speed over ground (SOG), heading (HDG), and nautical timestamps (UTC).
- **Casing Rules:** Telemetry tags and status badges employ uppercase text with expanded tracking (`0.06em`). Numerical vessel metrics maintain tabular numerical figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout adopts an edge-to-edge geospatial canvas model where telemetry cards, vessel inspectors, and the floating navigation dock hover over primary map viewports.

- **Grid Model:** 12-column dynamic grid on desktop viewports (>1024px) with fixed-width overlay sidebars (380px inspector drawer, 280px filter rail). On tablet and mobile viewports, the interface converts to unified bottom drawer overlays and a compact bottom dock.
- **Rhythm & Internal Density:** Internal telemetry cards use compact 8px base units, allowing dense instrumentation without crowding. Outer canvases leverage generous padding to prevent visual clashes with map controls.
- **Z-Index Architectural Stacking:**
  - Base: Geospatial Map Canvas (`z-0`)
  - Sub-layers: Route Polylines and Heatmaps (`z-10`)
  - Floating Panels: Telemetry & Deck Panels (`z-20`)
  - Command Bar & Navigation Dock: (`z-30`)
  - Overlays & Modal Trackers: (`z-40`)

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is defined by physical layering: frosted translucent sea-glass elevated over sage terrain, bordered by thin coastal outlines. Heavy black drop shadows are avoided in favor of diffuse environmental shadows tinted with marine slate.

- **Level 1 (Subtle Telemetry Tile):**

  - Border: 1px solid `rgba(82, 96, 88, 0.12)`
  - Shadow: `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(18, 23, 20, 0.04), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(18, 23, 20, 0.02)`

## Shapes

The interface embraces organic curvature inspired by hull dynamics and radar sweeps. Rounded pill contours eliminate harsh visual interruptions across maps and data overlays.

- **Dock & Pill Controls:** Pure pill radius (`9999px`) applied to navigation bars, segmented filters, and action triggers.
- **Large Cards & Inspector Panels:** 32px continuous curvature (`rounded-3xl`) for primary floating modules, providing structural softness over map tiles.
- **Inner Data Cells & Telemetry Chips:** 16px (`rounded-xl`) to 20px (`rounded-2xl`) for internal groupings, sub-cards, and data badges.

## Components

### Floating Navigation Pill Dock
- **Structure:** Floating horizontal pill centered at the bottom or top-center of the viewport. Height 56px, fully rounded (`rounded-full`), padded with 6px internal clearance.
- **Styling:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` surface with 20px blur, thin coastal border `rgba(82, 96, 88, 0.15)`.
- **States:** Active tab switches to solid `#121714` with `#FFFFFF` text and a subtle warm amber indicator dot (`#F59E0B`). Inactive tabs remain `#526058` with smooth background hover (`rgba(18, 23, 20, 0.05)`).

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Deep Sea Charcoal (`#121714`) fill, white text, pill-shaped (`rounded-full`). On hover: subtle scale `1.01`, background lightens to `#1C231F`. Active: scale `0.98`.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Ultra-clean white (`#FFFFFF`) with 1px border `rgba(82, 96, 88, 0.18)` and text `#121714`. On hover: border `#121714`.
- **Telemetry Action (Amber Beacon):** Filled `#D97706` with white text for alert triggers, route locks, or active tracking mode.

### Telemetry Cards & Vessel Inspectors
- **Container:** Pure `#FFFFFF` or `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.92)` backdrop, 24px–32px corner radius, 20px padding.
- **Header:** Vessel name in `headline-sm`, MMSI/IMO tags in JetBrains Mono (`telemetry-mono`) in `#718277`.
- **Data Matrix:** Grid of 2 or 3 columns with muted labels (`label-sm`, `#718277`) stacked above prominent tabular metrics (`body-lg`, `#121714`).

### Chips & Telemetry Status Badges
- **Nominal/Active:** Pill shaped, background `rgba(13, 148, 136, 0.10)`, text `#0D9488`, with a pulsing 6px circle indicator.
- **Caution/Underway:** Pill shaped, background `rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.10)`, text `#D97706`.
- **Dark Metric Tag:** Solid `#1C231F` background, text `#FFFFFF`, mono coordinates.

### Form Inputs & Search Fields
- **Search Pill:** Height 48px, fully rounded, pure white background, recessed inner icon, subtle border `rgba(82, 96, 88, 0.20)`. Focus ring: 2px solid `#121714`.
- **Segmented Range Sliders & Time Scrubber:** Sage track (`#CCD9CF`), amber scrubber fill (`#D97706`), deep charcoal drag handle with fine tactile white center.

### Radio Buttons & Checkboxes
- **Checkbox:** 20px rounded square (`rounded-md`), border `rgba(82, 96, 88, 0.3)`. Checked state: `#121714` fill with pure white tick mark.
- **Radio:** 20px circle, 2px stroke. Selected state: solid outer stroke with centered 8px amber or charcoal core.
